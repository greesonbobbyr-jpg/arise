# Arise

Personal strength-training PWA. Logs workouts Hevy-style, then tells you in plain English how much stronger you got and what to do when a lift stalls. Single user, no backend, all data on device.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole app: UI, styles, logic |
| `core.js` | Exercise library, strength standards, all analysis math (pure functions) |
| `characters.js` | The characters: images and their muscle shapes (generated by `tools/prepare-characters.py`, see `art/README.md`) |
| `anatomy.js` | The traced muscle template (MuscleMap, MIT, see THIRD_PARTY.md; built by `tools/build-anatomy.js`). Not loaded by the app: `tools/prepare-characters.py` fits its muscles onto the art |
| `art/` | Character art: the source sheets (`art/src`) and the 12 images the app loads (`art/chars`) |
| `standards.js` | Strength standards by bodyweight (generated by `tools/build-standards.js`, see THIRD_PARTY.md) |
| `titles.js` | The 125 titles (achievements) as data rows, and the stats they are checked against |
| `system.js` | The System: notification window, title emblems and reveal effects (canvas and SVG, no assets) |
| `sfx.js` | Sound effects, synthesized with Web Audio (no audio files) |
| `qr.js` | QR code generator for the invite panel (qrcode-generator by Kazuhiko Arase, MIT) |
| `sw.js` | Service worker: precaches the shell so the app opens offline |
| `manifest.json` | PWA manifest (installable, standalone, dark) |
| `icons/` | 192, 512 and 512-maskable icons |
| `tests.js` | Math test suite (`node tests.js`) |

## Deploy to GitHub Pages

1. Create a repo and push these files to its root (or a `docs/` folder).
2. Settings → Pages → Source: the branch and folder you pushed to.
3. Open the Pages URL in Chrome on Android, then Menu → Add to Home screen (or use the Install button on the Profile tab).

Everything is relative-pathed, so it works from a subfolder like `username.github.io/arise/`.

The service worker is network-first: every open fetches the live files (bypassing the HTTP cache) and only falls back to the cached copy when offline or when the network takes more than 3.5 s. A deploy shows up on the very next open. Bump `APP_VERSION` in `index.html` (shown at the bottom of Profile) and `VERSION` in `sw.js` when you release, so you can tell versions apart.

## What's in v1.1

- **History**: recent workouts on the home screen, full history grouped by month, tap to expand sets, delete with confirmation.
- **Charts**: dated e1RM chart per lift with a dashed baseline; tap a lift card for its last eight sessions.
- **Rank means strength**: the Hunter letter (E to S) comes from your best strength tiers, not XP. The home card tells you exactly which lift and what e1RM gets the next rank. Level stays XP-driven.
- **Daily quest**: one concrete target a day (keep the streak, beat a shadow, break a stall, or just show up). Completing it in a workout adds 30 XP.
- **Shadow row**: each exercise shows your all-time best set. A row turns green as you type numbers that beat it.
- **Rank up**: a full-screen moment when your rank letter rises or a lift crosses a tier line.
- **Rank card**: Profile → Make card renders a shareable image with rank, level, streak and top lifts.
- **Streak shield**: one missed week per calendar month is forgiven, derived from your log, nothing to manage.
- **Screen wake lock** during workouts (toggle in Profile) and an optional rest-over notification. The notification is best-effort when the screen is off because Android may stop the service worker timer; the wake lock is the reliable fix.

## Try it with demo data

Open `index.html?demo` on a fresh install to seed eight weeks of sample workouts (it skips the setup screen). Use Profile → Erase all data to clear it.

## More than one person

There are no accounts. Each person installs Arise on their own phone and their log stays on that phone; nobody sees anyone else's.

- **First-run setup** ("Awakening"): name, choose your character (male or female), bodyweight with lb/kg, age and workouts per week, then an ARISE moment. It only appears when there is no log; anyone with a log or routines from before is treated as set up (`profile.setupDone`). Everything stays editable in Profile, where the Male/Female switch also changes the character, the standards and the title targets.
- **Characters**: illustrated hunters, male or female, front and back, in three builds. The head, hair, hands and shoes are the drawing (in shadow); below the neck the body is the System's muscle map, every muscle outlined in cyan with no gym clothes. Each muscle trained this week lights up in the System's green, brighter with more sets, with a fibre texture and a glow, on a summoning circle inside a status frame. Tap a muscle to name it. `charSVG(sex, view, opts)` in `index.html` draws one; `art/README.md` explains the art and how the muscle map is fitted onto it.
- **The body grows with rank**: lean at E and D, athletic at C and B, powerful at A and S (`C.buildFor`). A rank-up that changes the build shows the new physique.
- **Shadow aura (a reward)**: the violet aura appears only when all 16 body-map regions have been trained in the last 7 days (`C.auraStatus`, `C.AURA_REGIONS` in core.js). Its strength grows with Hunter rank. Progress shows "Shadow aura · 12 / 16 trained this week · Missing: …", and the workout that completes the set plays a one-time AWAKENED moment.
- **Invite a hunter** (Profile): Share link opens the phone's share sheet (or copies the link), and QR code shows a scannable code for the app's own address, drawn on the phone by `qr.js` (qrcode-generator, MIT). The browser tests decode it with jsQR (test-only, `e2e/vendor/`).
- **Fair titles for women**: same 125 titles, ids and grades. The weight-based ones (`FEMALE` in `titles.js`) get women's targets at about the same percentile among women who lift as the men's targets among men; rep feats count at lighter weights (`FEAT`). `ARISE_TITLES.forSex(sex)` returns the set to use. `node tools/women-titles.js` prints both percentiles for every title; a unit test keeps them within 12 points (the top rungs, where the data runs out, are exempt).
- **iPhone**: Safari has no install button and can clear a website's data after about a week without a visit, so iPhones that have not added Arise to the Home Screen get a card with the steps (Home, dismissible for 14 days; always in Profile).
- **Backups**: with 10 or more workouts and no backup in 30 days, Home suggests Export backup (Later snoozes 14 days). Reminder state lives in `state.hints`.

```
bash e2e/run.sh      # unit tests + browser flows in headless Chrome at 390px and 360px
```

The browser flows need Node 22+ and Chrome (`e2e/serve.js` is the local server). Set `CHROME_PATH` if Chrome is not in the default Windows location. `bash e2e/run.sh titles sound` runs only the named flows. Screenshots land in `%TEMP%\arise-shots`, and the sound flow writes a WAV preview of every sound to `%TEMP%\arise-shots\sounds`.

## Strength tiers: who you are compared to

Tiers compare your estimated 1RM with **people who lift and log their lifts**, same sex and bodyweight, using Strength Level's published standards (about 48 million logged lifts). Beginner, Novice, Intermediate, Advanced and Elite mean stronger than 5, 20, 50, 80 and 95% of those lifters. This is not the general population: most people do not lift, so Novice is already above the typical untrained person.

- Thresholds are real weights at your bodyweight, interpolated between five bodyweight anchors per sex (`C.thresholds`). Heavier lifters need a lower ratio, which a flat bodyweight multiple gets wrong.
- Dumbbell lifts compare per dumbbell. Pull-ups, chin-ups and dips compare total load (bodyweight + added). Push-ups have no published weight standard, so they show progress only.
- The "Est. stronger than ~N% of all men/women" line is an estimate, not a measurement. It blends the lifter percentile with CDC data on how many adults strength train at all (`C.popEstimate`), and only appears from Novice up.
- Every lift card has "How this is measured" with the five thresholds in lb at your bodyweight.

**e1RM**: each working set gets `weight × (1 + reps/30)` with reps capped at 12. A session counts its highest set, so later sets never lower it. "Current" is the best session in the last 28 days. The **shadow** is your all-time best set on a lift; the app tells you whether your last session beat it.

`node tools/build-standards.js` regenerates `standards.js` and validates it. `node tools/spot-check-standards.js` prints lookups next to published values.

## Calendar

The Calendar tab plans workouts ahead. Drag a routine from the tray onto a day, or tap a day and pick one (search matches routine and exercise names). It saves to that day only. On a planned day you can turn on **Repeat every [weekday]**, move it by dragging, skip one occurrence, or stop the repeat. **Auto-fill** offers a weekly pattern (a routine per weekday, repeating until changed) or a rotation (an ordered loop with rest days, written day by day for 4, 8 or 12 weeks). Days show planned, done, missed, or an unplanned workout. The home screen shows what is planned today with a Start button and a strip for the week.

Data: `state.plan = { days: { 'YYYY-MM-DD': routineId | '' }, repeats: [{ id, dow, routineId, from, until? }] }`. A day entry beats a repeat; `''` skips one occurrence. Pure helpers live in `core.js` (`plannedFor`, `dayStatus`, `monthGrid`, `fillRotation`, `planStats`). There are no timed reminders: a web app cannot fire reliable notifications while closed without a server.

## Titles and the System

Achievements are titles. There are 125, in 17 categories and four grades (36 common, 37 rare, 36 epic, 16 legendary), from "finish your first workout" to "deadlift 585 lb".

- **One row per title** in `titles.js`: `[id, name, category, grade, glyph, mark, stat, target, condition, flavor]`. A title is earned when `stats[stat] >= target`. `ARISE_TITLES.stats(...)` builds every stat from the log in one pass. To add a title, add a row; `node tools/titles-report.js` prints the counts and flags duplicates.
- **Lifted-weight titles use weight actually logged** for at least one rep (bench 225, deadlift 405, 100 lb dumbbells), never an estimate. The few that use estimated 1RM say "(estimated 1RM)" in their condition.
- **Every title has its own emblem**: a frame for the grade (hexagon, strutted hexagon, winged shield, twelve-point star), a glyph for the kind of feat, and a mark such as `405`, `x20` or `52W`. A test fails if two emblems match.
- **Sealed until opened**: the workout summary and the home screen only say a System notification is waiting, and the Titles screen shows `???`. Opening one plays the reveal (seal break, light pillar, shockwaves, sparks, decoded name); higher grades hit harder. With more than three waiting, the window offers **Reveal all** (a fast cascade, tap to skip, then a summary) or one at a time, and any card in a long queue can reveal the rest at once. Sealed state lives in `game.unseen`.
- **Titles screen** (Profile → Titles): grouped by category, filters for earned, locked and each grade, and a progress bar on every locked title ("163 lb / 185 lb"). Tap an earned title to replay its reveal.
- **History counts**: when the catalog grows (`TITLES_VERSION` in `index.html`), the whole log is re-checked once on the next open and anything earned arrives sealed.
- **Sound**: notification chime, seal break scaled by grade, cascade ticks, accept and rank-up. Profile has an On/Off switch and a Test button. Nothing plays while logging sets. Effects are skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

`index.html?demo&sealed` seeds two sealed titles; `?demo&sealedall` seeds a full batch to try Reveal all. `#titles` opens the Titles screen directly.

## How weight is entered

Each exercise has a fixed convention, shown on the workout screen as the weight column header and a hint under the name:

| Convention | Examples | Column |
|---|---|---|
| Total load | Barbell, machines, one-stack cables, goblet squat | `lb` |
| Per dumbbell | Dumbbell press, curls, rows, lunges | `lb/DB` |
| Per side | Cable fly on two stacks, single-arm cable work | `lb/side` |
| Added weight | Pull-ups, dips, push-ups | `+lb` |

Progress, PREVIOUS, the shadow row and PRs compare in the entered unit. Volume counts both hands or sides; strength standards for dumbbell lifts are per dumbbell, the same way they are logged. Custom exercises pick their convention when created.

Covers Epley (170×5 → 198, 170×8 → 215, +8.6%), bodyweight loads, the 14-day baseline window, stall detection and every prescription branch, percentile interpolation and caps, the readout generator, and the streak/level math.
